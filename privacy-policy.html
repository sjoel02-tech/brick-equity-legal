Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

At Brick Equity, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we handle your information when you use our application.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information necessary to manage your LEGO collection, including:

  • Inventory data (Set numbers, quantities, and purchase prices).
  • Anonymized usage statistics and crash reports to improve app performance.
  • Account information if you choose to sync your data across devices.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the core services of Brick Equity, such as tracking market value, calculating portfolio equity, and managing your minifigure census.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. We only share anonymized data with automated pricing services to fetch the latest market values for your sets.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, export, or delete your collection data at any time directly through the app settings.
